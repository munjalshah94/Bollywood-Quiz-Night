How to play: controls

Keyboard

Space Enter
Start the show or a round, reveal the answer on a clue, start the charades timer
Esc
Go back one step: answer or clue, then board, then menu, then home
M
Open the round menu
H
Show the next hint (it asks “Are you sure?” first)
R
Restart the charades timer
?
Open this guide (home page)

On screen

Tiles
Tap a tile to open its clue. Grey tiles are already played; tap one again to replay it.
Reveal answer
Shows the answer. Back to board returns to the tiles.
Hint
Reveals one hint at a time, after you confirm.
Pass
Hands the clue to the next team for a +10 bonus. If every team misses, it can go round again at half points.
Mark unused
Opened a tile by mistake? Put it back on the board.
Menu
The button at the top right always returns to the round menu.
Scoreboard
Use plus and minus to score, or type a score. “Tap = ±” sets the step, “Teams” sets 2 to 6 teams, and names are editable. On a phone, tap “Show scores”.
Reset game
Clears scores and played tiles after a confirmation.
